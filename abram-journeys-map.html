Genesis names the places but not the roads, so these routes are traditional reconstructions following the river valleys and trade roads of the time. Several locations (especially Ur, Sodom, and Beer-lahai-roi) are debated by scholars; markers show the most common identifications. Mileage is a rough estimate.
